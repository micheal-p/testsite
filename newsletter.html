Questions

Before you subscribe.

How often will I hear from you?

Periodically, driven by what has actually changed rather than by a fixed calendar. In practice that has meant a briefing every one to two weeks. We do not send filler.

Do I need a NEIIA account?

No. The briefing is open to anyone. Access to the modules themselves is separate — see the account guide or contact the team.

Is this the same as a press release list?

No. Press and media enquiries are handled through the contact page, selecting "Media". This list is an operational briefing, not a publicity channel.

Can my whole team subscribe?

Yes, and each person should subscribe individually so that each holds their own unsubscribe right under NDPA 2023. Do not add colleagues on their behalf.

Are past issues available?

An archive is not yet published. Until it is, material platform and regulatory changes are also recorded in the help centre guides.

Something else?

Talk to the Administration directly.
