Edu Center

See the platform, screen by screen

Five of the nine modules have walkthroughs today. Each one follows the product in the order you actually meet it, with the real screens at every step where a screen has been captured. For the short written reference on any module, use the help centre.

Start with Deal Room Help centre

The five walkthroughs

Pick the one that matches what you are trying to do.

Module 01

Energy Assets

The on-platform marketplace for electricity, gas, and renewable carbon credits. Anonymous order books, standardized contracts, settlement routed through the energy databank.

2 screens · walkthrough
Module 02

Deal Room

SEC Nigeria-licensed marketplace for tokenised capital raises. Issuers list CSAFE, Bond, and Equity Subscription deals; verified investors subscribe in NGN with full KYC, bilateral agreements, and PIN-gated Data Vault custody.

3 screens · walkthrough
Module 03

LP Portal

Investor relations and reporting for fund managers. Send capital calls, push distributions, surface a live portfolio to every LP, and keep the document vault audit-tight.

6 screens · walkthrough
Module 04

Apex AI

The platform's intelligence layer. A corpus-bound assistant that reads your live portfolio across seven signals — and can act inside Deal Room without you leaving the chat.

Written walkthrough · screens to come
Module 05

NEFUND

The umbrella framework. One product — Deal Room — runs the full lifecycle: source, structure, raise, close, administer. Six stages, six vehicle wrappers, two cameras (GP raising / LP investing).

3 screens · walkthrough

Which one to read first

If you want to subscribe to a live raise

Start with the Deal Room walkthrough. It covers onboarding, the KYC tiers, what each tier lets you subscribe, and what happens to your money between reservation and close.

If you want to trade energy commodities

Energy Assets is the marketplace for electricity, gas and carbon. It is separate from the equity side.

If you are raising or running a fund

Read NEFUND for the framework, then the Deal Room for lifecycle mechanics, then LP Portal for how you report to investors afterwards.

If you already have a portfolio to read

Apex AI sits on top of everything else and answers questions against your own positions.
